04Hiring for Culture — New Interviews

Presents: AnneFLMs

What it is

Trial days are sunsetting soon. In their place: a 30-minute culture-based phone screen run by the recruiting team using our CHOICES values framework, followed by a competency-based in-person interview with the hiring manager — with real interview training to back it up.

What's in it for you

  • No more burning a crew slot on a trial-day no-show.
  • The culture screen happens before a candidate reaches your branch — you interview people who already fit.
  • BMs get trained on structured interviewing — a skill you keep for your whole career.

What's coming

Application tightening (we get ~17,000 applicants — the funnel is getting smarter, not just bigger) and continued tuning of the tested culture questions.

CHOICES · Communicate

"Describe a time your first attempt to teach or coach someone didn't work. What did you change, and what was the result?"

Listen for: clear, specific examples that feel natural to them — not slogans.

CHOICES · Ownership

"Have you ever missed a deadline or fallen short of an important goal? How did you handle it, and what did you learn?"

Listen for: owning it vs. getting defensive — we can teach skills, not values.

CHOICES · Support

"Tell me about a time you stepped in to support a co-worker who was falling behind. How did you manage your own work while helping them?"

Listen for: generosity with their own time, and how they kept their own work on track.

Attendance & punctuality

"If you woke up feeling tired or just not feeling it, what would go through your mind about whether to come in or not?"

Listen for: how they think about the team counting on them.

From Anne's tested Culture Assessment Interview: interviewers pick 1–2 questions per CHOICES value, rate each − / ± / +, and tally — 45–55 is a strong hire signal. Values first: hire for who they are, train the rest.
