In an effort to address infractions like rolling stops, distracted driving, and improper following distance, was going out on the road with my drivers and showing them exactly how sensitive the cameras were and giving them real-time examples of what would be counted as a driving infraction.

Driver Safety Coaching/Retraining
SG ★
Super Green: 90% or better coachings issued and retraining completed
Green: 80% or better coachings issued and retraining completed
Red: Less than 80% coachings issued and retraining completed
Results
100%
How it happened
My approach to this was very simple. I was tracking all metrics for the Einstein Games very closely, seeing what affected our score, whether it was negative or positive, and who did it. Obviously drivers score is a big one; I checked it daily when I came in. If I saw our score had dropped, I'd investigate the cause, identify the person responsible, and have coaching ready for them before they came into the office that morning.
What went well
Staying on top of it like that, and addressing any misconduct as it happens, helps the team understand the severity of driver safety, and it helps keep it at the forefront of their mind every time they get behind the wheel.
What I'd do differently
On two-truck jobs, I paired the lead mover with the lower driver safety score behind the lead with the stronger driving record. This allowed the lower-scoring driver to mirror safe following distances, speed management, lane selection, and overall driving habits in real time, creating a practical coaching opportunity while reducing risk for the crew

Biggest Constraint & Biggest Opportunity

You'll discuss these in the position breakouts — bring a proposed solution for your constraint.
Biggest constraint
My biggest constraint this quarter was personnel. We were essentially building a new branch with a new management team, and roughly 85% of our moving staff had little to no experience. The first half of the quarter was defined by constant hiring, onboarding, and turnover, making it difficult to establish consistency and build momentum.

Successfully navigating those challenges required balancing the day-to-day demands of the operation while remaining composed, making sound decisions under pressure, and continuing to develop a brand-new team. It was mentally and physically demanding, but laying that foundation has positioned us far better for the future than where we started the quarter.
Biggest opportunity
One area I'd like to focus on in the next quarter is generating more self-sourced leads by building strategic partnerships with apartment communities, realtors, and other key businesses in the Tampa Bay area. We finished Q2 in a much stronger position than where we started, but much of the quarter required Steven and me to focus on stabilizing operations. While we achieved strong results, we were often reacting to immediate challenges instead of proactively refining our processes and pursuing growth opportunities.

Now that the branch has a stronger foundation, we have the opportunity to invest more time in community outreach and relationship building. Expanding our local network and establishing strategic partnerships will increase brand visibility, create long-term referral channels, strengthen our presence in the Tampa Bay market, and ultimately drive sustainable revenue growth.
