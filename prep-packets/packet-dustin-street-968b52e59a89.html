Improve with Enthusiasm4One thing I try to do consistently is genuinely ask for coaching. I also consistently ask for documentation that would hold up to later review. The one thing I have to watch is analysis paralysis because sometimes I keep digging when there is already enough information in front of me to make the call and move forward.

It is not my defining strength, but it is something I am intentional about when things are running smoothly.
