What I'd do differently
No specific experiment run this quarter beyond working the leads as they came. Two next-quarter bullets: (1) triage fresh leads first instead of oldest-first, testing the theory that freshness drives conversion; (2) verbalize estimates on calls rather than defaulting to email, carried over from Goal 1.

Biggest Constraint & Biggest Opportunity

You'll discuss these in the position breakouts — bring a proposed solution for your constraint.
Biggest constraint
Our biggest constraint this busy season was probably hiring enough movers. We had more potential customers than we could service. Probably one of the big challenges will be how to swell our ranks quickly for the busy season while making sure that our standards and culture don't suffer as a result.
Biggest opportunity
Towards the middle/end of Q3 is probably when we'll start to see less job, and therefore, a smaller moving team. The best thing to strive for is making sure we retain our best movers, and take to opportunity a small branch size affords us to really push on EMC standards and culture. Basically, if a mover isn't a good fit and unable to adapt, they shouldn't be a part of the team.
