Einstein Games - Overall Score AccountabilityCRITICAL
R
Super Green: Branch Average total score is 8 or higher
Green: Branch Average total score is 7 or higher
Red: Branch Average total score is less than 7
Results
Branch average score 6.0, against a written bar of 7+ for Green. Reviews (4/13) and Damages (0/13) were the two categories that dragged the average down.
How it happened
Reviews suffered because roughly 50 guys were being scored and too many had zero 5-star reviews, pulling the average down despite others reviewing consistently. Damages hit zero because of high damage volume through May/June, driven by 20+ new crew members coming on with no Field Manager in the field to train/check them for a third of the quarter — a known gap from a management shift between Dallas-area branches — compounded by running more crews and jobs daily than ever before.
What went well
Attendance scored 8/13, tied for top in the company, driven by hiring dependable, reliable guys rather than any specific process. Driver safety trended up through the quarter as the new operations manager grew into the role, driven by more frequent check-ins with management and crew, keeping it visible at morning huddles and Training Day, and more consistent follow-through on warnings/coaching.
What I'd do differently
Mostly routine management this quarter — day-to-day volume (personnel, trucks, dispatch, sales) absorbed all available time while covering the FM gap and onboarding new crew, leaving no room to test new approaches on this goal. Next quarter: individual recognition/incentive for guys earning the most 5-star reviews, and reviving a team-based competition model for damage reduction with monthly/quarterly rewards.

No new method tested this quarter. Next quarter: fully utilize dedicated sales blocks and treat sales as a standing daily/weekly priority — not just during blocks — especially once hiring and truck volume ease up.
