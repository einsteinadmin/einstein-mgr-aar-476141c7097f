Biggest Constraint & Biggest Opportunity

You'll discuss these in the position breakouts — bring a proposed solution for your constraint.
Biggest constraint
We discussed this for a bit when Cam & Paul were here for the World Cup. We hired an unbelievable number of people this summer. We were busier than I can ever remember in my 5+ years working here. With that being said, quality started to slip. I think the way we function as a business is 1 of 1, and there aren't many things I would change. One specific thing, though, would be overall mover training for the entire team. We have 1 day each month where we have every employee on each team in a room to go over company news, policy updates, etc. I personally feel like & have felt like for a very long time, that for the movers' sake we should be minimizing more of those things I just mentioned, and pushing more for real full-blown training across the entire team. Moving is what we do, and sometimes it feels like we as a company put the actual capability of our movers on the back end of things that need to be discussed. I feel like it is all of our responsibility as managers to make sure that all of our employees are in the best possible position to go into the field & take care of the customers if we're going to charge the amount we do for the number of people we send. I understand we have our OB training & other processes to help with this stuff, but I can confidently say after 5 years of doing it at a high level, those types of things don't just make a good mover. Jobs will be different based on the crew that is on it, and that is inevitable. The mindset that we've always had, being "any 2 movers should be able to do any job that 2 other movers did," is just not a realistic expectation to have. Across any industry, you have high performers & people who just naturally pick things up quicker/easier, but not only that, some people are actually just better at this job than others. I think if not this quarter, then one of the very near quarters, we really would benefit from making "mover skill" a big company push. It looks like that's the plan based on the "Mover Quality" flywheel. My main thing is that in a time in the world where everything is so expensive, we need to be able to justify $105/hr for a copilot who started 2 weeks ago, and with our current process, it puts us in a very tight spot when the customer can very clearly see that said copilot is new & not giving the same output as their partner. Much love & respect.
Biggest opportunity
I kind of incorporated my opportunity & constraint into one. I think finding a way to utilize our current leads that are in good standing across performance metrics to train our new guys is our biggest opportunity this quarter.
